# Design audit against the UX principles (2026-10-08)

The existing plans were written by different sessions before [`principles.md`](principles.md)
existed. This is the check for the "different departments" problem: where a plan contradicts
a principle, duplicates another plan's mechanism, or misses something a principle needs.
**All findings were applied to the plans on 2026-10-08.** The two user decisions: capabilities
is the cornerstone (finding 6), and drivers phases 4-5 were rewritten for userland driver hosts
(finding 21). Keep this file as the worked example of an audit (P5.5); the next audit is a new file.

Severity: **conflict** (the plan does the opposite of a principle), **gap** (the plan is
silent where a principle needs something), **priority** (right idea, wrong place in the order),
**stale** (the doc no longer describes reality).

## `ai/llm-as-ui-plan.md`

1. **Gap (P7, P8, B5).** The agent "sees" through screenshots and acts with keys/pointer. With
   the semantic tree (B5) it reads roles and names and acts on them: cheaper, testable,
   and the same tree serves accessibility. Change: make the tree the primary "see/act" tool,
   screenshots the fallback. No decision needed.
2. **Conflict (education evidence).** The demo ("writes a 3D fractions game and opens it")
   matches the format the research warns about only if the game is a free chat; a generated
   microworld with control of error is fine. Change: point the demo at
   [`../ai/education.md`](../ai/education.md)'s flow. No decision needed.
3. **Gap (P4.1).** "Keep destructive tools behind user confirmation." Under P4 the answer is
   reversibility (run in draft, U1; generations, U3), not dialogs (P9: confirmations train
   users to click Allow). Change: destructive tools act inside a draft the user commits.
   Needs U1 or a simpler per-app-dir snapshot first.
4. **Gap (P3.2).** Memories and the learner model are sent to an API. Keep them local; send
   only what a request needs, visibly (P1.3). Already flagged as a caveat there; make it a rule.
5. **Duplication risk (P5).** App directory + manifest here, apps-as-directories in U4, the
   repo in `software-on-demand.md`: one app format, defined once (in
   `software-on-demand.md`), referenced by the others.

## `ai/capabilities-plan.md`

6. **Priority (P6).** "Good idea, not urgent, not on the critical path". Under the principles
   it is backbone B3: sandboxed previews (F2), the shared repo, native generated code,
   userland drivers and kids' accounts all depend on it. **Decided:** the cornerstone, ahead of
   the other `docs/ai/` plans.
7. **Gap (P6.2).** IOMMU is not mentioned. Any userland driver doing DMA needs it
   (`software-on-demand.md`, driver ladder). Add as a step after NX.

## `ai/threejs-runtime-plan.md`

8. **Conflict (P7, B5, accessibility).** "Draw UI in the scene" (the DOM-gap option 1) makes
   generated apps invisible to a screen reader and to the agent that must test them. Change:
   the `ui.text/ui.button` helper emits semantic-tree nodes. No decision needed.
9. **Fits.** The runtime as the sandbox is P6.1. Linking NVK statically is fine for now; under the linking policy (`userland/roadmap.md` step 2) NVK becomes a shared platform library later.

## `userland/roadmap.md`

10. **Tension (P3.4, P6).** Step 2, dynamic linking, is right for running foreign Linux
    binaries (compatibility). Revised 2026-10-08: policy "static, except the platform"
    (musl, NVK: shared, content-addressed, pinned per generation), with a page cache for
    immutable files so sharing saves memory; see `userland/roadmap.md` step 2. (An earlier
    version of this finding claimed static linking prevents slopsquatting: wrong, that attack
    happens at build time.)
    No decision needed unless the user wants dynamic native apps.

## `userland/init-plan.md`

11. **Fits P6.5** (supervision restarts services). **Gap (P1.2):** when a service dies the
    supervisor should record why (exit status, signal, last log lines) where the UI can show
    it. Small addition to step 7 (logs).

## `gui/gui-plan.md` (phase 4 decisions, 2026-09-26)

12. **Fits:** server-side decorations (a hung client still has a working close button, P6.3);
    resize on release; the panel as a separate client (P6.5); the launcher list as a text file
    (P7.2).
13. **Gap (P2.1, P2.4)** (applied: `gui-plan.md` "Pendiente según los principios de UX"). No focus policy is written: when a new window gets focus, and
    whether a click on an inactive window passes through. Add C1's rules.
14. **Gap (P1.2).** "If the client ignores `close`, there's `^\` or `kill`." Add: after a
    timeout the frame says the client is not responding and what it waits on (E2), and offers
    to end it.
15. **Priority.** "Out of scope: clipboard, drag and drop." Under P5.2/P6.1 these are the
    powerbox (K1) and the system clipboard (K2), not optional extras. Keep them out of
    phase 4, but design them as one mechanism when they come.

## `gui/compositor-visual-plan.md` (Luna)

16. **Risk (P8.2).** Luna's menu columns are translucent white (75%) and light blue (66%) with
    text on them; the taskbar is 78% glass. This is the Liquid Glass failure mode. Change:
    add C7 (measured contrast, auto-darkening) before more glass. No decision needed.
17. **Risk (P9.1).** Step 5, 3D icons "turning or lifting on hover": motion pulls primary
    attention. Keep motion on direct hover only, never ambient. Note in the plan.
18. **Fits P2.6:** the theme is chosen by the user and both looks are kept.

## `gpu/hw-cursor-plan.md`

19. **Supports P2.5.** The pointer leaves the frame loop, so it moves at the display's rate
    whatever the compositor does. No change; it gains priority under P2.

## `drivers/roadmap.md`

20. **Stale.** Phase 5 frames NVIDIA as "running the proprietary blob or the open modules
    through a LinuxKPI-scale layer". The project wrote its own driver (`nvgpu`, GSP-RM, NVK) and
    reached Vulkan; the doc doesn't say so.
21. **Conflict (P6.2).** Phase 4 aims at a stable **in-kernel** driver ABI for foreign drivers.
    The crash evidence (third-party kernel drivers: 70% of Windows crashes; CrowdStrike) argues
    for foreign and generated drivers **in userland**, behind capabilities and the IOMMU, with
    class drivers (P5.3) first. **Decided:** phases 4-5 rewritten: userland driver hosts
    (capabilities, device fds, IOMMU, supervision), the risk ladder, LinuxKPI only inside a
    userland host; hand-written drivers may stay in the kernel.
